# Breaking Bad

> A story of transformation, morality, and consequences.

---

**Title:** Breaking Bad  
**Genre:** Crime, Drama, Thriller  
**Created by:** Vince Gilligan  
**Release:** 2008–2013  

---

## Summary
When chemistry teacher **Walter White** is diagnosed with cancer, he begins producing meth to secure his family’s future — dragging his former student **Jesse Pinkman** into a dangerous criminal world.
